# 2026 Fantasy-Football Top 200

**Current through September 5, 2026.**

## Format assumption

This is for a **12-team, one-QB, half-PPR redraft league**, four-point passing touchdowns, one regular flex, snake draft. It ranks individual **QB/RB/WR/TE players**; D/ST and kickers are addressed separately.

**Do not use this order for superflex or two-QB leagues.** Quarterbacks would move dramatically higher.

## Research basis

- Baseline: [FantasyPros half-PPR expert consensus](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/half-point-ppr-cheatsheets.php), refreshed September 5 with 133 experts.
- Projection comparison: [DraftSharks’ live half-PPR rankings](https://www.draftsharks.com/rankings/half-ppr), [PFF’s half-PPR top 200](https://www.pff.com/news/2026-fantasy-football-rankings-half-ppr-top-200-for-drafts), and [ESPN’s September 2 PPR cheat sheet](https://g.espncdn.com/s/ffldraftkit/26/NFL26_CS_PPR300.pdf?adddata=2026CS_PPR300).
- Draft cost: [FFToday’s September 3 ADP](https://www.fftoday.com/rankings/26-adp-half-ppr.html), combining Underdog and Yahoo drafts.
- Preseason usage: starter snaps, routes, depth-chart order and backfield rotations from [DraftSharks’ preseason analysis](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/preseason-takeaways), rather than exhibition box-score production alone.
- Historical validation: 2025 [RB](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/reports/leaders/half-ppr-rb.php?year=2025), [WR](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/reports/leaders/half-ppr-wr.php?year=2025), [QB](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/reports/leaders/qb.php?year=2025), and [TE](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/reports/leaders/half-ppr-te.php?year=2025) scoring.

Notable 2025 half-PPR results:

- Christian McCaffrey led RBs with **365.6 points**, followed by Jonathan Taylor at 339.3, Bijan Robinson at 331.3 and Jahmyr Gibbs at 328.4.
- Puka Nacua led WRs with **310.5**, ahead of Jaxon Smith-Njigba at 300.4.
- Josh Allen scored 374.6; Drake Maye was close behind at 359.0.
- Trey McBride scored 252.9, more than 86 points ahead of the next TE.

## Most important late adjustments

- **Puka Nacua remains No. 4.** His psoas/groin soreness is a real Week 1 concern, but he was [running at high speed and progressing](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/puka-nacua-making-progress-204759353.html). The 2025 WR1 result supports keeping him in the elite tier.
- **Christian McCaffrey is No. 5.** He returned to team work after missing August 8–24 with tightness. The absence was described as [planned workload management](https://www.nfl.com/news/christian-mccaffrey-absence-from-practices-was-part-of-the-plan-from-the-jump), but his 413 touches last season increase durability risk.
- **Chase Brown falls to No. 21.** He was RB8 in 2025, but preseason usage showed Samaje Perine taking a meaningful portion of early-down and third-down work.
- **Malik Nabers is No. 28.** Elite upside, but he is returning from an ACL tear and subsequent cleanup procedure. The Giants have been optimistic about Week 1, but the recovery is not risk-free ([NFL.com](https://amp.nfl.com/news/giants-gm-joe-schoen-on-wr-malik-nabers-recovery-i-still-think-he-ll-be-fine-week-1-vs-cowboys)).
- **Jeremiyah Love, Emeka Egbuka, Ashton Jeanty and Breece Hall are discounted** for high-ankle, toe, ankle and groin issues respectively. The latest status summary is [here](https://ftw.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/2026/09/05/7-fantasy-football-injuries-egbuka-jeanty/91611147007/).
- **Rhamondre Stevenson rises to No. 61; TreVeyon Henderson falls to No. 87.** Henderson had not practiced since August 24 and remained out on September 5 ([NFL roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-news-roundup-latest-league-updates-from-saturday-sept-5)).
- **MarShawn Lloyd rises to No. 82. Josh Jacobs is off the top 200.** Jacobs cannot practice or attend games while on the Commissioner’s Exempt List, and there is no firm return date ([NFL.com](https://amp.nfl.com/news/nfl-places-packers-rb-josh-jacobs-commissioner-exempt-list)).
- **Patrick Mahomes is QB14, not because he will necessarily miss Week 1.** He worked with the first team throughout camp and [appears on track to start](https://amp.nfl.com/news/chiefs-qb-patrick-mahomes-appears-on-track-to-start-week-1-vs-broncos), but the knee reconstruction may reduce rushing and one-QB leagues have plentiful replacements.
- **George Kittle, Zach Charbonnet and Jordyn Tyson carry substantial availability discounts.** Tyson is on IR and could miss September and part of October ([PFN](https://www.profootballnetwork.com/fantasy-football/jordyn-tyson-fantasy-prediction-2026/)).
- Positive developments: Zay Flowers returned to practice; Luther Burden returned from his groin issue; Bucky Irving is full-go following shoulder surgery; Tucker Kraft and Tyler Warren had encouraging camp participation.

**Legend:**

- **†** = meaningful injury or availability risk.
- **↑** = elevated because of health, preseason role or depth-chart movement.

## Players not to take at current market cost

- **Josh Jacobs:** current ADP around 89 is unusable while he is on the exempt list. Leave him undrafted in ordinary redraft leagues.
- **Jordyn Tyson:** market price around 115 is too early for a rookie already on IR. No earlier than the last two rounds; easier to roster with an IR slot.
- **Jeremiyah Love:** market ADP around 28 does not sufficiently reflect a high-ankle sprain. My board: 44.
- **Chase Brown:** ADP 14 is too aggressive given Perine’s preseason usage. My board: 21.
- **Makai Lemon:** ADP around 94 is expensive after a rusty preseason and uncertain immediate volume. My board: 119.
- **Jayden Reed:** market price around 86 is too early after he was removed for some two-receiver formations. My board: 105.
- **TreVeyon Henderson:** do not draft him as an immediate RB2. His ankle and lost practice time make Stevenson the safer opening-week Patriot.

## Position strategy

- **Rounds 1–4:** Build around RB/WR. Take Bowers or McBride only when the elite RB/WR tier has dried up.
- **QB:** Allen is viable around the late second/early third. Lamar around the fourth is better value. Otherwise wait for Maye, Burrow, Hurts, Daniels, Herbert, Lawrence or Dak.
- **TE:** Bowers/McBride are the premium tier. Loveland/Warren are the middle-round targets. If those prices rise, wait for Kincaid, Goedert, Ferguson, Andrews or Juwan Johnson.
- **Bench RBs:** Prioritize paths to a starting workload—Lloyd, Mike Washington, Emmett Johnson, Kaelon Black and Malik Davis—over low-ceiling veteran receivers.
- **D/ST and kicker:** Use your final two selections. Preferred D/ST group: **Houston, Rams, Denver, Seattle, Philadelphia**. Preferred kickers: **Brandon Aubrey, Cameron Dicker, Ka’imi Fairbairn, Cam Little, Jason Myers**.
- **Draft-day rule:** Recheck Puka, Jeanty, Breece, Love, Egbuka, Henderson, Nabers and Kittle immediately before selecting them. One practice or IR designation can move each player by a full tier.
